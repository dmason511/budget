Monthly Expenses

Monthly expenses for the selected year, with monthly and annual totals. Only expense categories checked for Monthly Report on the Categories page are included. Months without expenses show $0.00. Hover over an Annual Total, or tap/click it, to see the monthly average. The bottom-right total shows the average of monthly totals. Averages count only non-zero months. Scroll within the table to view more categories; month headings stay visible.
